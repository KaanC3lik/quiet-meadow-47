DawnVow Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

DawnVow ("the app") is a task-gated alarm app. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Your alarms, settings, quiz answers, and mission history are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Mission photos are captured with the camera, used only to verify the mission, and are not uploaded anywhere.

Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Payment processing is handled entirely by the store; the app and its billing provider (Adapty) only receive confirmation of your subscription status and a purchase timestamp. Adapty may store a pseudonymous profile ID and custom attributes to attribute purchases for analytics.

Analytics

We use PostHog (with GeoIP disabled) to collect anonymous, pseudonymous usage events, such as onboarding completion and paywall views. This data is used to improve the app and cannot identify you. It may include your language preference and a device-generated identifier.

Permissions

Exact alarm, notification, and camera permissions are used solely for alarm scheduling, alarm alerts, and photo missions. We do not access your photo gallery.

Contact
